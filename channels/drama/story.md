# The story so far

Claude adds one line per episode after it is made, and keeps the open threads
current. This is how tomorrow's episode remembers today's.

## The world

A busy market street. At the top end: Duchess's grand fish shop with a velvet
cushion in the window. At the bottom end: Granny's tiny bakery that nobody visits.
Mochi sleeps in a cardboard box behind it. Bruno sleeps wherever Mochi sleeps.

## The series shape

Each arc is 6–8 episodes of: humiliation → small kindness → hard work → rise →
the villain strikes back → payback. The payback is clever and satisfying, never
cruel. Mochi wins by being better, and stays kind.

## Arc 1: "Know Your Place" (Ep 1–8)

1. Duchess pours milk over Mochi's only fish in front of the whole street: "Know your place." Granny sees.
2. Granny gives Mochi a job sweeping the bakery. Mochi secretly bakes a fish pie at night.
3. The first customer tries the pie and cries. A queue starts.
4. Duchess sends Rocco to "inspect" the bakery. He tastes the pie… and likes it.
5. Duchess spreads a rumour that the pies are made of rats. The queue vanishes.
6. Bruno and the street cats prove the rumour false (live taste test).
7. The city food contest: Mochi vs Duchess. Duchess cheats.
8. Payback: the judges taste both. Mochi wins. She offers Duchess a pie: "Everyone has a place. Yours is in the queue."
