## The house

A small, cosy house with a garden. Granny lives alone with Mochi (cat) and
Bruno (dog). Pip (mouse) lives in the wall behind the fridge. Mochi's throne is
the top of the sofa. Bruno's bed is by the door, and he is not allowed on the sofa.
